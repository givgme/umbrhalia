    Method note

    Where these bands come from

    The calculation is our own, made in Wolfram Language with JPL DE440 ephemerides: dates, type, duration, saros and the polygon of each band. It was checked eclipse by eclipse against the NASA/GSFC catalogue (Espenak and Meeus, Five Millennium Canon of Solar Eclipses): from 2026 to 2126 the 151 central eclipses match in date, type and saros. Countries and places: intersection of each band with Natural Earth.

    The duration shown is NASA's. Our own calculation gives totalities a few seconds longer, because of the lunar radius each source uses. An asterisk marks our own figure where NASA gives no duration.

    After ~2060, the time and east-west position of each band are uncertain: they depend on ΔT, the difference between atomic clock time and the time set by the Earth's rotation, which cannot be predicted decades in advance. One minute of ΔT shifts the band ~0.25° in longitude. On a world map this changes nothing; to decide whether a city on the edge falls inside, it does.

    For hybrids, countries and places are only those of the total stretch. For the detail on Mexico (local durations, band edges) there is a separate calculation of our own.
