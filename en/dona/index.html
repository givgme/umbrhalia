What we do with your donation

A total eclipse lasts a few minutes

What surrounds it takes months: bringing the sky to those who cannot travel to the shadow, and keeping what people feel when the light goes.

Workshops in public schools

Each workshop goes through four moments: understanding, sensing, recognising the history and answering.

The installation

A room where the light dims, the sound changes and stars appear at noon.

The eclipse of 2 August 2027

Travelling to the band of totality to document it and gather testimonies.

The other three destinations of your donation

Telescope observation

Watching the Sun with a filter, or the Moon at night, with someone who explains what you're seeing.

Tactile and accessible material

Reliefs of the phases of the eclipse and pieces you can listen to, so that no one is left out because they cannot see.

Ohr's sound stories

Recording and producing Ohr's voice so that each story can be heard in full, without depending on the images.

Notice

The design does not protect your eyes. What protects you is a filter that meets ISO 12312-2. Only during totality, and only inside the path of totality, is it safe to look without a filter. Before and after, never.
