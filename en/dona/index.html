Notice

The design does not protect your eyes. What protects you is the filter that meets the ISO 12312-2 standard. Only during totality, and only inside the path of totality, is it safe to look without a filter. Before and after, never.

Umbrhalia in one paragraph

Umbrhalia by LFAE is a transmedia project of art and science about total solar eclipses and the memory of those who live them: Ohr's stories, a guide to watching the sky safely, a digital museum, a participatory archive, workshops and an immersive installation. It is produced by the Laboratory of Physics, Art and Existence (LFAE), active since 2021, on the way to the total eclipse of 2 August 2027.
