What Umbrhalia is

A transmedia narrative universe to preserve what solar eclipses leave in people's memory

Ohr, the consciousness of matter that has watched the universe take shape, can make contact with us only in the few minutes when the Moon fully covers the Sun. Each person who lives an eclipse and tells it keeps that window open before it closes for good.

How do you keep an experience that lasts minutes and marks a life?

Eclipses of the coming decades are predicted to the second. What people feel in front of them stays in no archive, and outreach usually explains the phenomenon and leaves the experience out.

One project, four values

Scientific

Calculated paths, safe observation and the mechanics of the sky, told rigorously.

Historical

More than a thousand years of records, from codices to chronicles, connected with today's testimonies.

Social

An open, accessible archive where anyone is a co-author.

Cultural

Art, narrative and collective memory around a phenomenon that many cultures have named and recorded.

Notice

The design does not protect your eyes. What protects you is the filter that meets the ISO 12312-2 standard. Only during totality, and only inside the path of totality, is it safe to look without a filter. Before and after, never.

Umbrhalia in one paragraph

Umbrhalia by LFAE is a transmedia project of art and science about total solar eclipses and the memory of those who live them. It brings together Ohr's stories, a guide to watching the sky safely, a digital museum of the eclipse in art and history, a participatory archive of testimonies, outreach workshops and an immersive installation. It is produced by the Laboratory of Physics, Art and Existence (LFAE), active since 2021, on the way to the total eclipse of 2 August 2027.
