ARTqueología del Cielo

Activity · oral storytelling

“El eclipse”, by Augusto Monterroso

A reading or performance of the story, followed by a conversation.

For
All ages
Where
Schools, and as a short theatre performance at outreach events
Moment
Recognise, and also the narrative experience

Augusto Monterroso, “El eclipse”, in Obras completas (y otros cuentos), Mexico City: Imprenta Universitaria, 1959.

To read the full story: Obras completas (y otros cuentos), Ediciones Era. The site does not publish the story.

A Spanish friar, about to be sacrificed by a Maya community, tries to save himself by threatening to darken the Sun.

The twist (spoiler)

His captors already predict eclipses with astronomical precision.

What Monterroso reverses

Alexios I Komnenos (1084), Columbus (1504) and Mark Twain (1889) tell the same scene: whoever predicts is European, and whoever is astonished is the other. Monterroso reverses it.

In ARTqueología del Cielo: 1084 · 1504 · 1889

After reading

A conversation about the astronomical knowledge of pre-Hispanic civilizations and the prejudices the story subverts. At the end, an open space: what new knowledge the story brings and which prejudice it questions.

Social theatre · in development

Social theatre performances with an adaptation of the story. An adaptation first needs authorisation from those who hold the rights to the work.

Astronomical observation

Telescopes and filters

Only during totality, and only inside the path of totality, is it safe to look without a filter. Before and after, never.

Glasses and handheld viewers must meet the ISO 12312-2 international standard. Binoculars and telescopes must have a solar filter from a reputable manufacturer attached to the front. How to look: Path, stop 5
