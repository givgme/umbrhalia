Privacy

What this site knows about you

Almost nothing, on purpose.

This site uses no cookies and collects no personal data.

We count visits with GoatCounter, a service that uses no cookies and does not store your IP address. It records the page you open, the site you came from, your type of browser and device, your screen size and your approximate country. That tells us how many people read us, not who they are. If your browser is set to “Do Not Track”, we do not count your visit.

Whatever you write in “Leave your word” stays in your browser and never reaches us.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the maps load from unpkg. Like any web server, those services receive your IP address and handle it under their own policies.

Before the shared archive opens, we will publish a full privacy notice, before asking you for any data.
