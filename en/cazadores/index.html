Eclipse chasers

Expeditions to the band of totality.

A total eclipse can only be experienced inside the path where the Moon's shadow falls. This branch gathers the journeys to be there and what each journey leaves in the archive.

Page in preparation. The 2027 and 2028 dates are eclipses, not confirmed expeditions.

LFAE expedition · 8 April 2024

Puente de Ojuela, Durango

The LFAE expedition watched the total eclipse from the Puente de Ojuela. Totality lasted 4 min 26 s.
